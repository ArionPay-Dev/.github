**ArionPay** is a non-custodial crypto payment gateway designed for modern merchants who demand absolute control over their revenue. We bridge the gap between global commerce and decentralized finance, ensuring funds move directly from customers to your wallet—no middlemen, no frozen balances, and no KYC for merchants.

---

### 🌟 Key Features
* **Non-Custodial:** We never hold your private keys or your funds. 
* **Zero-KYC Onboarding:** Start accepting payments instantly with zero friction.
* **0% Fees:** Enjoy your first **$6,000** in transactions completely fee-free.
* **Multi-Currency Support:** Accept over 50+ cryptocurrencies globally.
* **Instant Settlement:** Payments are confirmed on-chain and land directly in your wallet in real-time.
